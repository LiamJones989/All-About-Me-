// 01 about

A little about me

I'm Liam. I'm someone who gets an idea in my head and usually wants to figure out how to actually build it. A lot of my time goes into coding, computers, electronics, servers, 3D printing, and experimenting with new technology.

I enjoy the entire process — starting with a blank screen, figuring out how something should work, breaking it a few times, fixing it, and eventually turning it into something people can actually use.

I'm especially interested in software development, engineering, AI, entrepreneurship, and building things that solve real problems.

I'm not trying to just learn technology. I want to use it to build something of my own.
